They Ask — You Answer 他們問 — 你回答（點 🔊 聽整組）

❓ Visitor: “What is this?”
🧑‍🏫 You: “This is a stone lion. It guards the gate.”
訪客：「這是什麼？」　你：「這是石獅，它守著大門。」
❓ Visitor: “How old is the shrine?”
🧑‍🏫 You: “It is more than 200 years old.”
訪客：「這座家廟有多老？」　你：「它有兩百多年歷史。」
❓ Visitor: “Whose family is this?”
🧑‍🏫 You: “It is the family of Wang De-lu, a great admiral.”
訪客：「這是誰的家族？」　你：「這是王得祿的家族，一位偉大的提督。」
❓ Visitor: “Can I take a photo?”
🧑‍🏫 You: “Yes, of course. Please be gentle and quiet.”
訪客：「我可以拍照嗎？」　你：「當然可以，請輕聲、小心。」
❓ Visitor: “Where is the temple?”
🧑‍🏫 You: “It is right next door. Follow me!”
訪客：「廟在哪裡？」　你：「就在隔壁，跟我來！」
🎭

At the Shrine Gate 在家廟門口 — 完整對話

Read it with a partner — one is the Visitor 🙋, one is the Guide 🧑‍🏫. Then swap!
兩人一組念念看——一個當訪客 🙋，一個當導覽員 🧑‍🏫，然後交換！

🙋 Visitor
Hello! What a beautiful old building. What is it?
你好！好漂亮的老建築，這是什麼？
🧑‍🏫 Guide
Welcome! This is the Wang Family Shrine. It is more than 200 years old.
歡迎！這是王氏家廟，它有兩百多年歷史。
🙋 Visitor
Wow! Who was the Wang family?
哇！王家是誰呢？
🧑‍🏫 Guide
Wang De-lu was a great sea-admiral. Our town, Taibao, is named after his title!
王得祿是一位偉大的水師提督。我們的鎮「太保」，就是用他的封號命名的！
🙋 Visitor
Amazing! Can I take a photo of the stone lions?
太驚人了！我可以拍石獅的照片嗎？
🧑‍🏫 Guide
Yes, of course. Please be gentle and quiet. Thank you for visiting!
當然可以，請輕聲、小心。謝謝你來參觀！
✏️

Check Yourself! 來檢查看看你學會了嗎

Q1
A visitor asks: “How old is the shrine?” You say…
提示：說它的年紀。
✅ Yes! Answer the question about age.沒錯！要回答「年紀」這個問題。
Q2
A visitor asks: “Can I take a photo?” A polite guide says…
提示：有禮貌地答應。
✅ Great! A good guide is polite and helpful.很好！好的導覽員有禮貌、樂於幫忙。
Q3
A visitor asks: “Whose family is this?” You say…
提示：這是哪一位提督的家族？
✅ Correct! The shrine belongs to admiral Wang De-lu's family.正確！家廟屬於提督王得祿的家族。
Q4
What is a friendly way to end a tour?
提示：導覽結束時的客氣話。
✅ Yes! End warmly: “Thank you for visiting!”沒錯！溫暖地結束：「謝謝你來參觀！」
🌟

Now It's Your Turn! 換你了！

Role-Play Challenge 角色扮演大挑戰

  • 1️⃣With a partner, act out the whole dialogue. 兩人一組，把整段對話演一遍。
  • 2️⃣Swap roles — now you are the other person! 交換角色——換你當另一個人！
  • 3️⃣Add one new question and answer of your own. 自己加上一組新的問與答。
  • 🏆Bonus: perform it for the class, or at the real shrine! 加分題：演給全班看，或到真正的家廟前演一次！
