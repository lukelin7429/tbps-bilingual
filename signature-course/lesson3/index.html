Lesson 3 · 第三課

Walking to the Shrine 走進王氏家廟

Open your eyes — today we look! · 張大眼睛，今天我們用「看」的！

👀 🚶 🏯 🔍

For teachers · 給老師： Lesson 3 of 6 (≈ 40 min) — the field lesson. Ideally walk the class to the shrine; if a visit isn't possible, use photos. Students learn to describe what they see with position and counting words, then record on the observation sheet. Tap 🔊 to hear English. 六課中的第三課（約 40 分鐘）——走讀課。最理想是帶全班走到家廟；若無法實地，改用照片。學生學會用「位置詞、數量詞」描述所見，並填寫觀察學習單。點 🔊 聽發音。

Build a Looking Sentence 組一個觀察句

I can see how many what where.
e.g. “I can see two stone lions at the gate.” 例如：「我看到大門口有兩隻石獅。」
There is / There are what where.
e.g. “There is a plaque on the wall.” 例如：「牆上有一塊匾額。」

I-Spy at the Shrine 家廟尋寶 — 找到了就大聲說出來！

Find each thing, then say its sentence out loud. Tap 🔊 to check.
找到每一樣東西，然後把句子大聲念出來。點 🔊 對答案。

🦁 I can see two stone lions at the gate.
我看到大門口有兩隻石獅。
🪧 There is a plaque on the wall.

我看到門上方有一個漂亮的雕刻。

那扇大門又紅又古老。

Check Yourself! 來檢查看看你學會了嗎

Q1
Two stone lions sit at the entrance. You say: “I can see two stone lions ______.”
提示：它們在大門口。
✅ Yes! The lions are at the gate.沒錯！石獅在大門口。
Q2
There is one plaque. Which sentence is correct?
提示：「一塊」用 There is。
✅ Right! One thing → There is.答對了！單一一樣東西用 There is。
Q3
Which word tells you where something is?
提示：哪一個是「位置詞」？
✅ Yes! “beside” tells you where. (“beautiful” and “old” describe.)沒錯！beside 說的是位置；beautiful、old 是形容。
Q4
A good observer first…
提示：觀察員要先做什麼？
✅ Yes! Look carefully and quietly, then describe.沒錯！先安靜仔細地看，再描述。
🌟

Now It's Your Turn! 換你了！

Young Observer Challenge 小小觀察員大挑戰

  • 1️⃣At the shrine (or in a photo), find 5 different things. 在家廟現場（或照片裡）找出 5 樣不同的東西。
  • 2️⃣Say a looking sentence for each one out loud. 為每一樣大聲說一個觀察句。
  • 3️⃣Write them on your observation sheet. 把它們寫進你的觀察學習單。
  • 🏆Bonus: which thing is your favourite? Tell a friend why, in English! 加分題：哪一樣是你最喜歡的？用英語告訴同學為什麼！
