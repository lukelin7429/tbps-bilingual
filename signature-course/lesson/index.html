The Shrine at the End of the Lane 巷子盡頭的那座家廟

Very close to our school, on Guangde Road, there is an old building called the Wang Family Shrine. 在離我們學校很近的廣德路上，有一座老建築，叫做「王氏家廟」。

It belongs to the family of Wang De-lu — a great sea-admiral born right here in Taibao, long ago. Our town is even named after his special title! 它屬於「王得祿」的家族——一位很久以前就出生在太保的偉大水師提督。我們的鎮，就是用他的封號命名的！

Children have played here for a hundred years. Today, you will learn to talk about it in English. 一百年來，孩子們都在這裡玩。今天，換你用英語來介紹它。

Say It Like a Guide! 像導覽員一樣說說看！

Welcome! This is the Wang Family Shrine.
歡迎！這是王氏家廟。
It is more than 200 years old.
它有兩百多年歷史。
Look — two stone lions guard the gate.
你看——兩隻石獅守著大門。
This plaque was given by a famous admiral.
這塊匾額是一位有名的提督送的。
Thank you for visiting. Any questions?
謝謝你來參觀，有任何問題嗎？

The Goddess Who Guards Children 守護孩子的神明

🌟

Right next door is Fuji Temple. Its goddess, the Seven Star Goddess, is believed to protect children. For a hundred years, families have come here to pray for their kids — maybe even for you! 就在隔壁是「福濟宮」。廟裡的神明「七星娘娘」，相傳會保護孩子。一百年來，許多家庭都來這裡為孩子祈福——說不定也包括你喔！

Check Yourself! 來檢查看看你學會了嗎

Q1
What is the name of the shrine near our school?
提示：它是哪一個家族的家廟？
✅ Yes! It is the Wang Family Shrine, the family hall of admiral Wang De-lu.沒錯！是王氏家廟，水師提督王得祿的家族祠堂。
Q2
Two ______ guard the gate of the shrine.
提示：守在門口的那兩隻石頭動物。
✅ Right! Two stone lions guard the gate.答對了！兩隻石獅守著大門。
Q3
Our town, Taibao, is named after a famous ______.
提示：王得祿的身分是什麼？(海軍的大將)
✅ Correct! Taibao is named after the admiral Wang De-lu's special title.正確！太保是用提督王得祿的封號命名的。
Q4
A good guide starts by saying…
提示：導覽員一開始會先做什麼？
✅ Yes! A guide welcomes visitors first.沒錯！導覽員會先歡迎訪客。

Be the Guide Challenge 小導覽員大挑戰

  • 1️⃣Read all 5 guide sentences out loud. 把 5 句導覽英語大聲念出來。
  • 2️⃣Choose your favourite treasure word and teach it to a friend. 選一個你最喜歡的寶物單字，教給一位同學。
  • 3️⃣Record a 30-second welcome, as if a visitor just arrived at the shrine. 錄一段 30 秒的歡迎詞，假裝有訪客剛到家廟。
  • 🏆Bonus: walk to the shrine with your class and say it for real! 加分題：跟全班走到家廟，真的說一次看看！
