The Goddess Who Guards Children 守護孩子的神明

Right next to the Wang Family Shrine stands Fuji Temple. Its goddess is the Seven Star Goddess — in Chinese, Qixing Niangniang. 就在王氏家廟旁邊，矗立著福濟宮。廟裡的神明是七星娘娘。

People believe she watches over children and keeps them healthy and safe. For more than a hundred years, families have come here to pray for their sons and daughters. 人們相信她守護孩子，保佑他們健康平安。一百多年來，許多家庭都來這裡，為自己的孩子祈福。

Maybe, long ago, someone walked here to wish a good life for a child — a child who grew up to be your grandmother, or your grandfather. Maybe even for you. 也許很久以前，曾有人走到這裡，為一個孩子祈求一生平安——那個孩子，後來成了你的阿嬤、你的阿公。說不定，也包括你。

Introduce It Politely 有禮貌地介紹

This is Fuji Temple, next to the shrine.
這是福濟宮，就在家廟旁邊。
People believe the goddess protects children.
人們相信這位神明會保護孩子。
Families come here to pray for their kids.
許多家庭來這裡為孩子祈福。
This belief is more than a hundred years old.
這個信仰有一百多年了。
Please be quiet and respectful inside.
在裡面請保持安靜、尊重。

A Good Guest at the Temple 當一位有禮貌的廟宇訪客

🙏

A temple is a special place for many people. A kind guide helps visitors enjoy it and respect it: speak softly, walk gently, look with your eyes, and ask before taking photos. 廟宇對許多人來說是個特別的地方。好的導覽員會幫訪客既欣賞它、也尊重它：輕聲說話、輕輕走路、用眼睛看、拍照前先問一聲。

✏️

Check Yourself! 來檢查看看你學會了嗎

Q1
Who is the goddess at Fuji Temple?
提示：和「星星」有關的名字。
✅ Yes! The Seven Star Goddess (七星娘娘).沒錯！是七星娘娘。
Q2
People believe the goddess especially watches over…
提示：這一課的主角是誰？
✅ Right! She is believed to protect children.答對了！相傳她守護孩子。
Q3
Inside a temple, a polite guide says…
提示：要尊重、要安靜。
✅ Yes! Be quiet and respectful.沒錯！要安靜、尊重。
Q4
“Families come here to ______ for their children.”
提示：在廟裡做的事。
✅ Correct! Families come to pray.正確！家庭來這裡祈福（拜拜）。

Now It's Your Turn! 換你了！

Gentle Guide Challenge 溫柔導覽員大挑戰

  • 1️⃣Introduce Fuji Temple to a visitor in 2 English sentences. 用 2 句英語向訪客介紹福濟宮。
  • 2️⃣Tell them one thing people believe — politely. 有禮貌地告訴他們人們相信的一件事。
  • 3️⃣Show one “good guest” manner (speak softly, ask before photos). 示範一個「好訪客」的禮貌（輕聲、拍照先問）。
  • 🏆Bonus: ask a grandparent what people wish for here. 加分題：問問阿公阿嬤，人們會在這裡許什麼願。
