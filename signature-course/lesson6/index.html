Lesson 6 · 結業課 · Final

Become a Guide! 當一位小導覽員

This is your moment — speak! · 這是你的時刻——開口吧！

🎤 🌟 🏯 🎓

For teachers · 給老師： The capstone (≈ 40 min). Each student gives a 1–2 minute guided introduction — at the shrine if possible, or in class with their card from Lesson 5. Use the rubric, cheer everyone on, and award the certificate. This is the moment the whole course has been building toward. Tap 🔊 to model the script. 結業課（約 40 分鐘）。每位學生做 1–2 分鐘的導覽——可在家廟現場，或在教室用第五課做的卡片。用評量表、為每個人加油、頒發證書。這是整套課一路鋪陳的高潮時刻。點 🔊 聽示範稿。

📜

Your Guide Script 你的導覽稿 — 把六課串成一段

Five simple steps. Tap 🔊 on each to hear it, then make it your own.
五個簡單步驟。點每一步的 🔊 聽聽看，再改成你自己的版本。

1. Welcome — “Hello! Welcome to the Wang Family Shrine.”
1. 歡迎——「你好！歡迎來到王氏家廟。」
2. Name & age — “It is more than 200 years old.”
2. 名字與年紀——「它有兩百多年歷史。」
3. Show 2 things — “Look — two stone lions guard the gate, and there is a plaque on the wall.”
3. 介紹兩樣——「你看，兩隻石獅守著大門，牆上還有一塊匾額。」
4. A story — “Our town is named after admiral Wang De-lu. Next door, a goddess is believed to protect children.”
4. 說個故事——「我們的鎮以提督王得祿命名。隔壁有一位相傳會保護孩子的神明。」
5. Thank you — “Thank you for visiting. I hope you enjoyed Taibao!”
5. 謝謝——「謝謝你來參觀，希望你喜歡太保！」

😊

🙏

Final Check — You Know It All! 最後檢測 — 你全都會了！

Q1
A good guide starts by saying…
提示：一開始要先做什麼？
✅ Yes! Start by welcoming your visitors.沒錯！先歡迎你的訪客。
Q2
Our town Taibao is named after…
提示：第一課的那位提督。
✅ Right! Admiral Wang De-lu.答對了！提督王得祿。
Q3
Two stone lions guard the…
提示：第三課的位置詞。
✅ Yes! They guard the gate.沒錯！牠們守著大門。
Q4
A great guide ends by…
提示：結束時要做什麼？
✅ Correct! Always end with a warm thank you.正確！永遠用溫暖的謝謝作結。
