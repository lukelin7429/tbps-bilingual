Our Leaders · 認識首長

From the Nation to Our Town 從國家到我們的小鎮

The public servants who support the education of Taibao’s children — from the President to the mayor of our own city. 支持太保孩子教育的首長們——從總統，到我們這座城市的市長。

Education is supported at every level, from the nation to the neighbourhood. We are grateful to the public servants whose care reaches the children of Taibao.
教育的支持來自每一個層級，從國家到鄉里。我們感謝這些首長，他們的關懷一路抵達太保的孩子。
