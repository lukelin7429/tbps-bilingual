Rooted in the arts 從藝術與國樂出發

Beyond his own school, Principal Chen has long served the wider field of arts education in Chiayi — and his heart is in music. He is the conductor and director of a teachers' Chinese-music orchestra, and his service roles all circle the same theme: that every child deserves the arts.

  • Conductor & instructor of a teachers' Chinese-music orchestra (華商教師國樂團指揮兼指導老師)
  • Deputy Convener, Chiayi County Arts & Humanities Curriculum Guidance Team (嘉義縣藝術與人文領域輔導團副召集人)
  • Supervisor & visiting committee member, Senior-Learning Education Guidance Team (樂齡教育輔導團督導及訪視委員)
  • Committee member, Arts-Talent Student Assessment under the Arts Education Act (藝術教育法藝術才能學生鑑定小組委員)

A principal who conducts an orchestra knows that a school, too, is many instruments learning to play as one. 一位會指揮樂團的校長知道：一所學校，也是許多樂器學著合奏成一個聲音。

That background is no accident at Taibao, a school known for its Chinese-music ensemble. Under a conductor's eye, the school's love of music and its reach toward English are two parts of the same idea — rooted in Taiwan, with eyes on the world.

這樣的背景，在以國樂團著稱的太保並非偶然。在一位指揮的眼裡，學校對音樂的熱愛、與向英語伸出的手，是同一件事的兩半——立足台灣，放眼世界。

A word on how we run this school 治校理念

I hold to two old and simple ideas: education for all, and teaching according to each child's aptitude. From them grows the campus I want for Taibao — a friendly place that is healthy, safe, and harmonious, rich in beauty and in the local character of this hundred-year town; and a school that grows together, sustainably, with its community.

Education is the foundation of a nation's development. A school does not develop alone: it asks its teachers, its staff, and its parents to take part in its long care. When all of us share one vision, we become a community of life — and within a fine school culture and a warm, friendly atmosphere, we build, together, a school that truly works.

我堅持兩個古老而簡單的信念：「有教無類」與「因材施教」。由此長出我為太保所期望的校園——一個健康、安全、和諧，富美感與地方人文色彩的友善校園；一所與社區共生共榮、永續發展的學校。

教育是國家發展的基礎。學校的發展不是一個人的事：它需要教師、職員與家長共同參與、永續經營。當我們全體秉持共同的理念，便形成一個生命共同體——在優質的學校組織文化、溫馨友善的校園氛圍下，齊心建立一所真正有效能的學校。

How I Lead

Three Principles

個人經營學校的三個理念
1

Students at the Centre 以學生為核心的教學氛圍

Students first, teaching first. We develop adaptive education that respects each child's differences and offers many ways to learn — teaching that is both local and global, rooted in Taiwan with eyes on the world.

堅持學生第一、教學優先，激發學生潛能；發展適性教育，尊重個別差異，提供多元學習；營造主動學習的契機；教學本土化、國際化，立足台灣，放眼世界。

2

Led by Communication 以溝通為導向的學校經營

Open channels so teachers, students, parents, and neighbours all take part. Administration supports teaching through clear, delegated, accountable work — cultivating a “learning school” with high care and a friendly, harmonious climate.

暢通溝通管道，讓師生、家長與民眾共同參與；落實行政支援教學，分層負責、充分授權、績效責任；培養「學習型學校」，提升教師專業；高關懷、高倡導，營造友善和諧的組織氣氛。

3

School & Community as One 學校社區化・生命共同體

School into community, community into school. We join parent and community resources to build the school's character, welcome local talents and volunteers, open our facilities as a learning centre, and grow intergenerational, lifelong learning — one community of life.

學校社區化、社區學校化：結合家長與社區資源建立學校特色，廣邀專業人才、成立志工團，重視親職教育，開放校園資源為多功能學習中心，發展代間教育、終身學習，營造學校與社區的生命共同體。

Where this leads 這會把我們帶往哪裡

“Rooted in Taiwan, with eyes on the world” is not a slogan for a banner; it is the thread that joins a Chinese-music ensemble to an English classroom. It is why this school is building its bilingual campus, and why its signature course teaches children to introduce their own town — the shrine, the temple, the museum at the gate — in two languages.

「立足台灣，放眼世界」不是布條上的口號，而是把一支國樂團連到一間英語教室的那條線。這就是為什麼太保正在打造它的雙語校園，也是為什麼學校的特色課程，教孩子用兩種語言介紹自己的家鄉——家廟、福濟宮、門口的故宮南院。
